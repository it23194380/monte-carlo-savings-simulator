Model Assumptions

This simulator estimates how monthly savings may grow under uncertain investment returns and inflation. All financial parameters are illustrative.

Simulation Duration 10 years (120 months)
Monte Carlo Simulations 10,000 paths
Annual Return Volatility 12%
Annual Inflation Volatility 2%
Savings Target LKR 2,000,000
Target Measurement Today's purchasing power

How the Simulation Works

Each month, savings grow or decline according to a randomly generated investment return. A fixed monthly contribution is then added at the end of the month. Inflation is simulated separately to calculate the purchasing power of the accumulated savings.

The success probability represents the percentage of simulated paths that reach the inflation-adjusted savings target after 10 years.
